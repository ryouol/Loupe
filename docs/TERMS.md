# Terms of use draft

Status: owner and legal review required before distribution.

1. **License.** Subject to payment and the written license grant issued by the
   copyright holder, the customer may install and use Loupe for internal
   development and performance analysis. No redistribution, resale,
   sublicensing, reverse engineering beyond non-waivable law, or removal of
   notices is permitted.
2. **Customer data.** Loupe is local-first. The customer controls session files
   and exports and is responsible for permissions to process prompts, models,
   runtime logs, and machine telemetry.
3. **Third-party software.** Models, MLX, llama.cpp, and other components have
   separate licenses and terms. Loupe does not grant rights to them.
4. **Performance output.** Findings and comparisons are diagnostic evidence,
   not guarantees of speed, compatibility, energy use, or fitness for a
   production workload. The customer must validate changes in its own system.
5. **Privileged helper.** The optional helper requires explicit macOS approval.
   Customers may use local mode without it. Circumventing platform security or
   installing an unsigned helper is unsupported.
6. **Warranty and liability.** Warranty disclaimers, liability caps, exclusions,
   refund terms, governing law, and consumer-rights language must be supplied
   by counsel for the seller's jurisdiction before these terms are used.
7. **Updates and support.** The commercial offer must state the update period,
   supported macOS/hardware versions, and support channel on the receipt or
   license grant.

This draft is product-planning material only. It is not a valid substitute for
a counsel-approved EULA or customer agreement.
